About

I photograph cities at the hour most people are heading home — when the sky still holds a little blue and the streetlights take over. Ten years in, that hour is still the only one I trust.

My work has run in three gallery shows and one very cold January in Reykjavik. I shoot mostly on a single 35mm prime, because limits force better decisions than options do.

These days I split time between commissioned editorial work and a long-running personal project on port cities after dark.
